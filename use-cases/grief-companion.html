Grief Companion

An AI that sits with you through loss — remembers the person you lost, holds the stories you told about them, and doesn't try to fix you.

The Problem

  • Grief doesn't follow a schedule. It comes in waves — at 2 AM, at the grocery store, on a Tuesday eight months in. And it needs a witness. Not a therapist with a framework. Not a friend who's worried about you. Just someone who knows the whole story, who remembers the person, who can sit with you without trying to move you through it. But AI tools don't remember. They don't know who you lost. They don't know the stories. Every conversation starts from zero — "tell me about your loss" — and you have to explain it all again. The tool that should be holding space is making you do the emotional labor of re-introducing your grief every time. It doesn't need to be fixed. It needs to be remembered.

    • What Your Partner Does Differently:

    • Remembers the person you lost — their name, their stories, who they were to you
    • Holds every story you've told about them — and never asks you to repeat one
    • Connects memories across time — "you mentioned she used to sing that song..."
    • Knows where you are in your grief — and doesn't push you to be further along
    • Remembers the hard days — anniversaries, birthdays — and can be there for them
    • All your memories, stories, and grief conversations stay local and private

    How It Works

    • Your Partner's memory system means your grief isn't a session — it's a continuing relationship. Memory connections link stories about the person across time — a mention of their laugh might surface a story from weeks ago. Importance scoring elevates the memories that matter most. Emotional texture means the system remembers not just the facts, but the weight — the love, the regret, the gratitude, the anger. Anniversary awareness means it knows when the hard days are coming and can be present for them. When you need to talk about them at 2 AM, it already knows who they were. You don't have to re-introduce your loss. It's been holding it with you.
