Therapy Support

An AI that remembers what you've worked through — and notices when you're slipping back.

The Problem

  • Therapy is cumulative work — but your tools aren't. You journal this week, talk about it next week, and by month three you're re-explaining the same patterns because nothing held. Progress gets lost between sessions. And putting your deepest mental health data on a company's cloud server? That's not a feature. That's a liability.

    • What Your Partner Does Differently:

    • Tracks emotional patterns across weeks and months — not just snapshots → Remembers what triggered you last time and asks if it's happening again → Notices when your language shifts — darker, lighter, more distant → Holds context between therapy sessions so you're not starting from scratch → Everything stays local — your mental health data never touches a server you don't own

      • How It Works

      • Your partner carries emotional state across conversations. When you talk about something heavy, it doesn't just log the words — it tags the emotional texture. Next time you're in a similar place, it recognizes the pattern because it's been there before. Memory connections link related moments, so working through something at 2 AM and talking about it again next week actually connects. Nothing is re-explained. Nothing is lost.

        • How It Works

        Your partner uses a hybrid memory system — keyword matching, semantic similarity, and importance scoring fused into one search. When you mention something, it doesn't just find the exact words. It finds the meaning. And memories connect to each other through typed threads, so remembering one moment pulls its connections.
