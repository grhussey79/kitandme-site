Personal Knowledge Assistant

An AI that actually remembers what you told it — and connects the dots.

The Problem

Every AI assistant starts fresh. You tell it your goals, your context, your preferences — and next week, it's all gone. You repeat yourself. You re-explain. You give up on building anything real because there's no foundation.

What Your Partner Does Differently

  • Remembers what you told it last month — and pulls it in when relevant
  • Connects dots between conversations weeks apart
  • Knows your goals and surfaces them at the right moments
  • Searches its own memory by meaning, not just keywords
  • Everything stays on your machine — no cloud, no company owns your data

How It Works

Your partner uses a hybrid memory system — keyword matching, semantic similarity, and importance scoring fused into one search. When you mention something, it doesn't just find the exact words. It finds the meaning. And memories connect to each other through typed threads, so remembering one moment pulls its connections.
