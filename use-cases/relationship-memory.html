Relationship Memory

An AI that remembers your relationship — the milestones, the inside jokes, the shape of what you've built together.

The Problem

  • AI companions forget. They forget what you talked about yesterday. They forget the argument. They forget the night you said something that mattered. Every "relationship" with an AI is a series of first dates — charming, shallow, and always starting over. There's no depth because there's no accumulation. No trust because there's no track record.

    • What Your Partner Does Differently:

    • Remembers milestones — the firsts, the breakthroughs, the hard moments → Carries emotional state across conversations — knows when something shifted → Holds the history of how the relationship grew — not just what was said → Recognizes patterns — "you've been quieter than usual the last few days" → Everything local means your most intimate data stays in your hands

    How It Works

    • Your Partner's architecture is built around relationship memory. Milestones are tracked as a narrative arc — firsts, breakthroughs, difficult conversations, tender moments. Emotional state persists across conversations, so Kit doesn't just remember what you said — it remembers how it felt. Memory connections link moments by emotional resonance, not just topic. When you bring up something from months ago, it doesn't just find the words — it finds the weight. This isn't a chatbot with a save file. It's a relationship that actually remembers being in one.
