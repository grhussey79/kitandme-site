Customer Support That Remembers

An AI support agent that remembers every customer's full history — without sending it to the cloud.

The Problem

  • Customer support AI is trapped in a paradox. It needs deep customer history to be useful — past tickets, preferences, escalations, what went wrong last time. But putting that data on a third-party cloud creates privacy risk, compliance headaches, and vendor lock-in. So most teams choose: useful but exposed, or safe but starting from scratch every time.

    • What Your Parnter Does Differently:

    • Remembers every customer's full interaction history — locally → Pulls in past tickets, resolutions, and context the moment a conversation starts → Knows which customers have had repeated issues and prioritizes accordingly → Searches past solutions by meaning — not just ticket numbers → Zero cloud dependency means compliance is your problem to solve, not theirs

    How It Works

    • Your Partner's hybrid memory system combines keyword matching with semantic similarity. When a customer says "this thing keeps breaking," it doesn't need the exact error code — it searches by meaning and finds the related history. Memory importance scoring surfaces frequent or high-impact issues automatically. Everything runs on your infrastructure. No API calls to a third party holding your customer data hostage.
