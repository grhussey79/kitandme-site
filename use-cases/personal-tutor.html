Personal Tutor

An AI that knows exactly where you're stuck — because it remembers where you've been.

The Problem

  • Every learning AI starts fresh. You mastered fractions last week? Too bad — this week it's explaining them again. You struggle with algebra but ace geometry? It doesn't remember that either. Adaptive learning without memory isn't adaptive. It's repetitive. You spend half your time re-establishing what you already know instead of building on it.

    • What Your Partner Does Differently:

    • Remembers what you've mastered and what you're still struggling with → Adapts lessons based on your specific learning patterns over time → Connects concepts across subjects — "you used this same approach in chapter two" → Tracks your learning style — visual, analogy-based, step-by-step — and sticks to what works → Progress persists across sessions, weeks, and months

    How It Works

    • Your Partner's memory system tags each learning interaction with context — the concept, the difficulty, the approach that worked. When you return to a subject, it loads your full history with that topic instantly. Semantic search means "that thing I couldn't understand in biology" finds the right moment. Memory connections link related concepts across subjects, so mastering one skill can reinforce another. The tutor grows with the student — not just within a session, but across an entire education.
